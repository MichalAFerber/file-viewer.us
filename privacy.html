Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

File Viewer is built to be private by default. Your files are read and rendered entirely inside your own browser — they are never uploaded.

The short version. We can't see your files. There are no accounts and no cookies. Analytics, where used, are anonymous and cookieless. Everything a viewer does happens on your device.

Your files stay on your device

When you open a file in any File Viewer, it is read and rendered locally by JavaScript running in your browser. Your files are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and nothing is stored on a server. Close or refresh the tab and the file is gone from memory — there is nothing to delete.

No accounts, no cookies

There is nothing to sign up for, and we do not set cookies or use tracking storage. Any preference a viewer offers (such as your chosen background color) is kept locally in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

Analytics

To understand roughly how many people use the sites, we rely on Plausible Analytics, self-hosted on our own server and cookieless. It records only aggregate, anonymized information — such as page views, referrer, country, and browser — and does not use cookies, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across other sites. No personal data is collected. If you block the analytics domain, the viewers work exactly the same.

Hosting

The sites are served as static files by Cloudflare Pages, which may keep standard, short-lived server logs (for example IP addresses) for security and abuse prevention, as described in Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

No third-party trackers

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no data brokers. Each viewer bundles all of its code into a single page and loads no third-party resources at runtime.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent version.
